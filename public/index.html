The Career & Job Search board is a mix of things you fill in here (In App) and things you ask me to do in a Claude Code chat (Claude Code Skill). For each skill, say the exact command shown, or just describe what you want in your own words — either works.

1. Set up your profile (this modal) In App

Personal Info and Work Details are the facts I autofill application forms with — contact info, work authorization, salary expectations, EEO answers. Fill these in once and every application draft reuses them.

Resume & Answers is where you upload past resumes (.docx) and I extract the text from them. Your first upload becomes the primary resume automatically; after that, mark whichever one you want with "Set Primary." The Resume Text box below auto-syncs to your current primary resume — that's the exact text I paste into free-text resume fields on application forms, so it's kept as one clean, coherent resume rather than every version merged together. For tailoring a resume/cover letter to a specific job (see #5 below), I read the full content of every resume you've uploaded, not just the primary one, so nothing you've ever written gets left out of the raw material I draw from.

2. Set your search criteria In App

Open the Career & Job Search project's Edit Project menu and fill in the "Job Search Criteria" section — target titles, industry, location, salary floor, and any extra job boards. This is what the daily search reads, so it knows what you're actually looking for.

3. Find jobs Claude Code Skill

Searches LinkedIn, Google Jobs, and any extra sources you listed, using your saved criteria, and adds new postings to the board: strong matches go in To Do ("Apply Today"), borderline ones go in the Future tab ("Worth Reviewing").

Say: /search-jobs

4. Suggest adjacent roles Claude Code Skill

Once you've uploaded at least one resume, this looks for roles you might be qualified for beyond what you're currently targeting (e.g. spotting Product Manager potential in Marketing Ops experience). Suggestions show up above in Suggested Adjacent Roles, where you can mark each one Interested or Not a fit.

Say: /suggest-roles

5. Tailor an application Claude Code Skill

For any specific job, drafts a tailored resume and cover letter — reworking your real experience to fit that listing (never inventing anything) — and saves the draft to that job's card for you to review. Open the task afterward and look for Application Status & Materials under Career Tracker Details.

Say: /tailor-application Acme Corp - Product Manager
(name the company/job, or just the task, so I know which one — I'll ask if it's ambiguous)

6. Apply to today's jobs Claude Code Skill

Opens each "Apply Today" listing and fills out the application form (using a tailored draft if one exists, otherwise your profile), then stops before the final submit — you always review and click submit yourself.

Say: /apply-to-jobs

7. Record progress & outcomes Claude Code Skill

Once you've clicked submit on something I filled out, or you hear back from a company — interview, offer, rejection, or silence — just tell me in plain language and I'll log it on that job's card (Outcome / Outcome Notes under Application Status & Materials). No slash command needed here, just say it naturally. Over time this is what shows which roles, companies, and tailoring approaches are actually working.

Say: "Mark Acme submitted" or "Acme rejected me" or "I have an interview with Acme"
